    2. Deconstructing TeamLink: The Core Mechanism

    n

    At its heart, LinkedIn TeamLink is a feature exclusive to the premium **Sales Navigator Advanced** and **Advanced Plus** subscriptions. Its primary function is to give you visibility into your team's collective network. It systematically scans the 1st-degree connections of everyone on your Sales Navigator contract and highlights when a colleague is directly connected to a person you're viewing.

    n

    The Core Mechanic: Revealing the "Warm Path"

    n

    Without TeamLink, you only see connections from your own profile. TeamLink gives you a "satellite view" of all the paths starting from your teammates' locations as well. When you search in Sales Navigator, a "TeamLink Introduction" tag appears on profiles where a connection exists, moving outreach from a game of chance to a game of strategy.

    4. A Practical Walkthrough: Using TeamLink Step-by-Step

    n

    Getting started is straightforward. The feature is designed to be largely automatic once enabled, working seamlessly in the background of your Sales Navigator workflow.

    nn
    n

    Step-by-Step Activation & Usage Guide

    n
    n
    Step 1: Administrator Setup

    The journey begins with the Sales Navigator admin, who must ensure the feature is active and licenses are correctly assigned.

    1. Navigate to Admin Settings in Sales Navigator.
    2. Find and verify that the master switch for TeamLink is enabled.
    3. Assign all available licenses to your team members.
    n
    Step 2: Finding Prospects with the TeamLink Filter

    This is where the magic happens. TeamLink integrates directly into the Sales Navigator search experience.

    1. Build your lead search using desired filters.
    2. In the search results, look for the "Spotlights" section.
    3. Select the "With TeamLink intro" filter to instantly refine your list.
    n
    Step 3: Requesting the Introduction

    Requesting an introduction is an art form. Make it easy for your colleague to help you.

    1. On the prospect's profile, identify the mutual connection.
    2. Click the message icon to contact your teammate.
    3. CRITICAL: Don't just ask "Can you introduce me?" Provide context and a pre-written, forwardable message.

    10. Trust & Privacy: Navigating the Human Element

    n

    A common question is about employee privacy. Are employees being forced to give the company access to their personal network? The answer is a clear no.

    n

    It's About Visibility, Not Control

    n

    When an employee opts in, they are **not** handing over their login credentials or giving the company the ability to message their contacts. They are simply making the *existence* of a 1st-degree connection visible. The employee always retains full control over their account and whether or not they facilitate an introduction.

    11. Comprehensive FAQ

    n
    n
    n
    1. Can I use TeamLink with a LinkedIn Premium or Sales Navigator Core account?

    No. TeamLink is exclusively available for subscribers of Sales Navigator Advanced (Team) and Advanced Plus (Enterprise) plans.

    n
    2. What if a team member leaves the company?

    When a team member's license is deactivated, their network is automatically removed from the TeamLink pool. The system stays current with your active team roster.

    n
    3. Where can I find official pricing?

    LinkedIn maintains an official comparison page for its Sales Navigator plans. You can find the latest details here: Official LinkedIn Sales Navigator Pricing.

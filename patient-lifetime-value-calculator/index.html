Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) About the Advanced Patient Lifetime Value (PLV) Calculator

nnnn

1. What is Patient Lifetime Value (PLV)?

Patient Lifetime Value (PLV) is the total estimated profit a healthcare practice can expect to earn from a patient over the entire duration of their relationship, including revenue from visits and referrals.

2. Why is PLV important for my practice?

PLV helps you:
Determine how much you can spend on patient acquisition (marketing) while remaining profitable.
Identify opportunities to increase revenue through retention and referrals.
Make data-driven decisions about service pricing and patient engagement strategies.

3. Who should use this PLV calculator?

This tool is ideal for:
Private healthcare practices (dentists, chiropractors, physical therapists, etc.)
Medical clinics and specialists
Wellness centers and holistic health providers
Healthcare marketers and consultants

4. What data do I need to use the calculator?

You’ll need:
✔ Average Revenue per Visit – What a typical patient pays per appointment.
✔ Average Visits per Year – How often a patient returns annually.
✔ Average Patient Lifespan (Years) – How long patients typically stay with your practice.
✔ Gross Profit Margin (%) – Your profit percentage after direct costs (e.g., 60%).
✔ Average Referrals per Patient – How many new patients each patient refers.
✔ Referral Conversion Rate (%) – Percentage of referrals who become paying patients.

5. How accurate are the results?

The calculator provides estimates based on your inputs. For maximum accuracy:
Use real practice data (from billing/CRM systems).
Adjust assumptions (e.g., referral rates) based on historical trends.

6. Can I save or share my results?

Yes! The PDF download feature lets you:
Save results for future reference.
Share with team members or financial advisors.
Use in business planning or loan applications.

7. How do referrals impact PLV?

Even a small number of referrals can significantly increase PLV. For example:
If a patient refers 0.5 new patients (lifetime) and your conversion rate is 50%, each patient indirectly adds 25% more profit to their lifetime value.

8. What if my practice doesn’t track referrals?

Estimate based on:
Patient surveys ("How did you hear about us?").
Reviewing new patient sources in your records.
Industry benchmarks (e.g., dental practices average 0.3–1 referral/patient).

9. How do I determine my Gross Profit Margin?

Calculate:

Gross Profit Margin (%) = [(Revenue – Direct Costs) / Revenue] × 100
Direct costs include supplies, lab fees, and staff time per visit.
If unsure, a typical range is 50–70% for many healthcare practices.

10. Why is my PLV $0 or negative?

Possible reasons:
❌ Profit margin is 0% or negative (check costs vs. revenue).
❌ Patient lifespan or visits/year is 0 (review retention strategies).
❌ Incorrect inputs (ensure all fields are filled correctly).

11. How can I increase my PLV?

Try:
✅ Boost retention – Improve patient experience to extend lifespan.
✅ Increase visit frequency – Optimize recall systems (e.g., reminders).
✅ Enhance referrals – Implement a structured referral program.
✅ Improve margins – Reduce costs or adjust pricing strategically.

12. Can I use this for multiple service types?

Yes! For varied services (e.g., exams vs. procedures), you can:
Calculate PLV separately for each service category.
Use weighted averages for a combined estimate.

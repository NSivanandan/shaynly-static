What Are QR Codes and Why Do They Matter?

nnnn

QR codes, or Quick Response codes, are two-dimensional barcodes that can be scanned with a smartphone camera. They direct users to a specific URL, making it easy to share information like website links, product pages, or contact details. But beyond their basic function, QR codes have become a staple in modern marketing for several reasons:

nnnn
    n
  • Convenience: They provide instant access to information with a single scan.

  • Versatility: Use them on business cards, flyers, packaging, or even digital ads.

  • Trackability: Many QR code tools allow you to track scans and gather data on user engagement.

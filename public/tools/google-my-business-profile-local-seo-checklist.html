1. Foundational Setup

Claim & Verify Your Profile High
Ensure you have full ownership and your business is verified.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

An unverified profile is invisible to customers and cannot be optimized. Verification (usually by postcard) is the non-negotiable first step that proves to Google your business is legitimate and located where you say it is.

Did You Know?

Google states that verified businesses are twice as likely to be considered reputable by consumers.

Ensure NAP Consistency High
Your Name, Address, and Phone number must be identical everywhere online.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

NAP consistency is a foundational ranking factor for local SEO. Inconsistent information across the web (your website, Yelp, Facebook, etc.) confuses Google and erodes trust, directly harming your ranking.

Critical Detail

This includes small details! "Street" vs. "St.", "Suite 100" vs. "Ste. #100" can be seen as inconsistencies. Pick one format and stick to it religiously.

Set Primary & Secondary Categories High
Choose the most specific Primary Category and add all relevant Secondary Categories.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

Your Primary Category is one of the most important ranking factors. It tells Google what your business *is*. Secondary categories help you rank for related, less direct searches.

Pro-Tip

Research your top competitors in the Map Pack. What is their Primary Category? You should likely use the same one if it's accurate. Be as specific as possible (e.g., "Italian Restaurant" instead of just "Restaurant").

Define Service Area or Physical Address High
Correctly set your business type: storefront, service-area, or hybrid.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

How you set this up fundamentally changes how you appear in search. Storefronts rank based on proximity to the searcher. Service-area businesses rank within the defined zip codes or cities. Getting this wrong can make you invisible to your target customers.

Warning

Do NOT set a physical address if customers cannot visit you there (e.g., your home office). This violates Google's terms and can lead to profile suspension.

2. Profile & Keyword Optimization

Write a Keyword-Optimized Business Description High
Use all 750 characters to describe your business, history, and services.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

While Google states the description is not a direct ranking factor, the text IS indexed. It helps Google understand the full context of your business and can help you appear for long-tail, semantic searches.

Pro-Tip

Write for humans first, but make sure to naturally include your main service keywords and location-based terms (e.g., "serving the Austin area"). Use the first 250 characters for your most important information, as this is what shows before the "More" button.

Fully Build Out Products & Services High
Add every single service and product you offer, with detailed descriptions and prices.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

This is a massively underutilized SEO goldmine. Each service and product you list is an opportunity to rank for that specific keyword. These often appear directly in your profile in search results, answering customer questions instantly.

Did You Know?

Adding detailed services can help you qualify for specific search features, like service quote requests directly from your GBP listing.

Upload High-Quality, Geotagged Photos Medium
Consistently add new, high-resolution photos and ensure they are geotagged.

Strategic Insight

Photos are a major engagement factor. Businesses with photos receive 42% more requests for driving directions. Geotagging your photos (adding location data to the image's EXIF data before uploading) provides an additional location signal to Google.

Pro-Tip

Aim to add at least one new photo per week. Cover all categories: exterior, interior, at work, team photos, and product/service photos. Name your image files with keywords before uploading (e.g., "best-coffee-shop-austin-tx.jpg").

Select All Applicable Attributes Medium
Thoroughly complete the Attributes section (e.g., "Wheelchair accessible", "Free Wi-Fi").
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

Attributes act as filters in Google Search and Maps. By selecting relevant attributes, you can appear in highly specific searches like "restaurants with outdoor seating near me". This is a simple way to capture long-tail search traffic.

3. Content & Engagement

Publish Google Posts Weekly High
Post updates, offers, or events at least once a week.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

Consistent posting is a powerful signal to Google that your business is active and engaged. The content of these posts is indexed and can appear in your GBP listing, providing timely information and keyword opportunities.

Did You Know?

Google Posts expire after 7 days (unless it's an event), so weekly posting is necessary to keep your profile looking fresh.

Seed and Answer Q&A Section Medium
Proactively ask and answer common questions about your business.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

The Q&A section is publicly visible and can be contributed to by anyone. By pre-filling it with your FAQs, you control the narrative and create a valuable, keyword-rich resource that can rank in search results.

Pro-Tip

Log into a personal Google account to ask a question, then log back into your business account to answer it. Upvote your own helpful answers to make them more prominent.

Enable Messaging Feature Low
Turn on messaging and respond to inquiries quickly.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

While not a direct ranking factor, fast response times are a positive engagement signal. Google displays your typical response time, so being responsive builds trust and can lead to more conversions.

4. Reviews & Reputation

Actively Solicit Reviews High
Create a system to consistently ask happy customers for reviews.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

Review quantity, velocity (how frequently you get them), and rating are among the most powerful local ranking factors. A steady stream of positive reviews tells Google your business is active, popular, and trustworthy.

Pro-Tip

In your GBP dashboard, go to "Ask for reviews" to get a direct shareable link. Add this to your email signature, receipts, or send it in a follow-up text/email after a successful transaction.

Respond to ALL Reviews High
Reply to every single review, both positive and negative, in a professional manner.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

Google has confirmed that responding to reviews improves your local SEO. It shows you are engaged with your customers. For negative reviews, a thoughtful response can turn a bad situation into a public display of excellent customer service.

Include Keywords in Review Responses Medium
When appropriate, naturally mention your services or location in your replies.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

The text in both reviews and your responses is indexed. This provides another opportunity to reinforce your business's relevance for specific services and locations.

Example

Instead of "Thanks for the review!", try "Thanks, John! We're so glad you enjoyed our deep dish pizza here at our downtown Chicago location."

5. Advanced & Technical SEO

Link GBP to Your Website High
Ensure your GBP "Website" button points to your most relevant local landing page.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

This link is a powerful signal. It connects your verified physical location to your digital one. For best results, link to a page on your site that is optimized for your main local keyword (e.g., a "Chicago Plumber" page, not just your homepage).

Embed Google Map on Your Website Medium
Embed a Google Map of your business location on your website's contact page.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

This creates another powerful connection between your website and your GBP listing, reinforcing your location's legitimacy and relevance to Google. It strengthens the location signal for your business.

Use Local Business Schema on Your Site Medium
Add LocalBusiness structured data to your website's contact or homepage.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

Schema markup is code that explicitly tells search engines detailed information about your business (like NAP, hours, etc.) in a language they can easily understand. This removes any ambiguity and strengthens Google's confidence in your data.

Build Local Citations Medium
Get your business listed with consistent NAP in relevant online directories (Yelp, etc.).
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

Citations are mentions of your business's NAP on other websites. A strong citation profile with consistent data across many reputable sites is a major trust signal that boosts your local ranking authority.

Analyze Performance Insights High
Regularly review the "Performance" section in your GBP dashboard.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

This is your report card from Google. It shows you exactly which search queries people used to find your business. This is invaluable data to refine your keyword strategy for your GBP, website, and content.

Set up UTM Tracking Links Low
Add UTM parameters to your website link in GBP to track traffic in Google Analytics.
Details & Insights

Strategic Insight

By default, traffic from your GBP listing can be hard to isolate in Google Analytics. Adding UTM parameters allows you to see exactly how many users are clicking your website link, what they do on your site, and whether they convert.

Example Link

https://yoursite.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gmb

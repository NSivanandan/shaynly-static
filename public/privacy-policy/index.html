Privacy Policy

Shaynly.com is owned and operated by Sivanandan. At Shaynly, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we collect from you when you use the Website, how we use that information, and your rights with respect to that information.

  1. Information We Collect When you use the Website, we may collect the following types of information from you:

(a) Personal information you provide to us directly, such as your name, email address, and any other information you choose to provide when you create an account on the Website, fill out a form, or contact us.

(b) Information about your use of the Website, such as your IP address, browser type, referring/exit pages, and operating system.

(c) Cookies, which are small data files that are stored on your device when you use the Website. We use cookies to improve your experience on the Website, to understand how you interact with the Website, and to personalize your experience.

  1. How We Use Your Information We may use the information we collect from you in the following ways:

(a) To provide and improve the Website, including to customize the content and advertisements you see on the Website.

(b) To communicate with you, including to respond to your inquiries and provide you with updates and information about the Website.

(c) To enforce our terms of use and other agreements with you.

(d) To comply with legal obligations, including to comply with applicable laws, regulations, and court orders.

(e) To protect our rights, property, or safety or the rights, property, or safety of our users or others.

  1. Your Rights You have certain rights with respect to the information we collect from you. These may include the right to:

(a) Access and obtain a copy of the information we hold about you.

(b) Request that we correct or update any inaccurate or incomplete information we hold about you.

(c) Request that we delete the information we hold about you.

(d) Object to the processing of your information for certain purposes.

(e) Request that we restrict the processing of your information.

(f) Withdraw your consent to the processing of your information (if we are relying on your consent to process your information).

If you would like to exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

  1. Data Security We take reasonable steps to ensure that your information is secure. However, no system can be completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information.
  2. Changes to this Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated Privacy Policy on the Website and indicate the date it was last updated. We encourage you to review the Privacy Policy periodically.
  3. Contact Us If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at sivanandan@shaynly.com.

Shaynly Opt-Out Preferences

We respect your privacy and understand that you may wish to opt out of certain data collection and usage practices. This opt-out page is designed to provide you with the necessary information and options to manage your preferences regarding the collection and use of your personal information on the Shaynly website.

Please carefully review the following options and select the ones that best align with your preferences:

  1. Cookies and Tracking Technologies:
    By default, Shaynly uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyze website traffic, and personalize content. If you prefer to opt out of these technologies, you can adjust your browser settings to disable cookies or use browser extensions/add-ons specifically designed for this purpose. However, please note that disabling cookies may impact the functionality and performance of the website.
  2. Email Marketing:
    If you no longer wish to receive promotional emails or newsletters from Shaynly, you can opt out by clicking the "unsubscribe" link provided in the emails you receive. This will remove you from our mailing list and you will no longer receive marketing communications via email.
  3. Third-Party Advertising:
    Shaynly may engage in third-party advertising networks and partners to display advertisements on our website. These partners may collect information about your browsing activities across different websites to provide you with personalized advertisements. If you prefer not to receive targeted advertisements from these partners, you can visit the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out page at http://optout.networkadvertising.org/ or the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) opt-out page at http://optout.aboutads.info/. These opt-out mechanisms will allow you to manage your preferences for targeted advertising across participating websites.
  4. Data Analytics:
    Shaynly uses analytics tools to collect and analyze data about website usage and performance. This information helps us improve our services and enhance the user experience. If you wish to opt out of data analytics, you can disable JavaScript in your browser or utilize browser extensions/add-ons that block analytics scripts.
  5. Data Sharing with Third Parties:
    Shaynly values the security and confidentiality of your personal information. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for their direct marketing purposes. However, we may disclose your information to trusted third parties who assist us in operating our website, conducting business, or providing services to you. If you have concerns about data sharing, please contact us using the information provided below.

If you have any questions, concerns, or would like to exercise your rights regarding your personal information, please contact our privacy team at sivanandan@shaynly.com. We will make reasonable efforts to address your requests and provide you with the necessary assistance.

Please note that opting out of certain practices may limit your access to certain features or functionalities on the Shaynly website.

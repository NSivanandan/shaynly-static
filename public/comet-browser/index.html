What is Perplexity Comet Browser?

Perplexity Comet represents a paradigm shift in web browsing, transforming the traditional browser from a passive information display tool into an active AI-powered assistant. Launched in 2025, Comet is built on Chromium but reimagines the entire browsing experience around artificial intelligence.

Revolutionary Concept

Unlike traditional browsers that simply display websites, Comet actively assists users in completing tasks, making decisions, and navigating complex workflows through natural language interactions.

Free Access

Originally priced at $200/month, Perplexity made Comet completely free for all users in October 2025, democratizing access to AI-powered browsing.

"Comet is designed to be a thought partner and assistant that removes the friction of context-switching between dozens of applications." — Aravind Srinivas, Perplexity CEO

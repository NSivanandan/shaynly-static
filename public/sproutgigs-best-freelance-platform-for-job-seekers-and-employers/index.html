How can I create an account on Sproutgigs?

Creating an account on Sproutgigs is quick and easy. All you need to do is go to the Sproutgigs website and click on the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner of the screen. Then, enter your name, email address, and create a password. Once you've filled in all the required information, click on "Sign Up" and your account will be created.

How do I post a gig on Sproutgigs?

To post a gig on Sproutgigs, you need to first create an account and sign in. Once you're signed in, click on the "Post a Gig" button in the top right corner of the screen. Then, enter all the required information about your gig, such as the job title, description, budget, and location. You can also add any additional details or requirements for the job. Once you've filled in all the necessary information, click on "Post" and your gig will be live on the Sproutgigs platform.

How does payment work on Sproutgigs?

Sproutgigs uses a secure payment system to ensure that both employers and freelancers are protected during transactions. When an employer hires a freelancer for a gig, they deposit the payment amount into an escrow account. The freelancer then completes the work and submits it to the employer for approval. If the work is satisfactory, the employer releases the payment from the escrow account and the freelancer receives the payment. If there are any issues or disputes, Sproutgigs has a dispute resolution process in place to help resolve the situation.

How does Sproutgigs ensure quality work from freelancers?

Sproutgigs has a strict vetting process for freelancers to ensure that only the best and most qualified candidates are listed on the platform. Additionally, employers are encouraged to leave feedback and reviews of freelancers they have worked with, which helps to build a community of quality freelancers on the Sproutgigs platform.

What are the fees for using Sproutgigs?

Sproutgigs charges a service fee of 10% on each gig posted on the platform. This fee is deducted from the payment amount before it is released to the freelancer. There are no additional fees for creating an account or browsing the available gigs on Sproutgigs.

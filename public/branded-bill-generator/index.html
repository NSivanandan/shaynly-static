Here are answers to common questions users might have about creating a branded bill with this tool:

nnnn

1. What makes a branded bill different from a regular invoice?

A branded bill includes your company’s logo, colors, and name, making it a personalized extension of your brand. Unlike a regular invoice, it’s designed to leave a lasting impression and reinforce your professional identity.

2. Can I use the Branded Bill Generator on my phone?

Yes! The tool features a responsive design that works seamlessly on both mobile devices and desktops, ensuring you can create branded bills anytime, anywhere.

3. Which currencies does the tool support?

The Branded Bill Generator supports multiple currencies, including USD ($), EUR (€), GBP (£), JPY (¥), INR (₹), AUD (A$), and CAD (C$). It adjusts decimal places automatically for accuracy (e.g., 2 decimals for INR, 0 for JPY).

4. How do I add my logo to a branded bill?

Simply upload your logo (recommended size: 300x100px) via the “Upload Logo” field. It will appear in the invoice preview and PDF, enhancing your branded bill’s professional look.

5. Why doesn’t my company name show up in the PDF?

If this happens, ensure you’ve entered your company name before generating the PDF. The tool includes a fix to capture dynamic content, so the name should appear if updated in the preview first.

6. Can I customize the colors of my branded bill?

Absolutely. Use the color picker to select your brand color, which will apply to key elements like headers and totals, giving your branded bill a cohesive look.

7. Is the Branded Bill Generator free to use?

The tool as provided is a standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript solution—free to implement on your own website or local environment. Additional hosting or premium features would depend on how you deploy it.

8. How long does it take to create a branded bill?

With its intuitive interface and real-time preview, you can create a fully customized branded bill in just a few minutes, from configuration to PDF download.

9. Can I switch between dark and light modes?

Yes, the tool offers a toggle to switch between dark and light themes, allowing you to tailor the preview to your preference or brand style.

10. What happens if I need to add more items to my branded bill?

Click the “+ Add Item” button to include additional line items. The preview updates instantly, and all items will appear in the final PDF.

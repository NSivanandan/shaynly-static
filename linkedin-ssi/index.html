What is the LinkedIn SSI Score?

n

The Social Selling Index (SSI) is a proprietary LinkedIn metric that measures your effectiveness as a social seller. It scores you on a scale of 1 to 100, based on your activities across four key pillars. Think of it as a credit score for your sales reputation on the world's largest professional network.

n

It's LinkedIn's data-driven assessment of your ability to establish your brand, connect with the right prospects, offer valuable insights, and build trusted relationships.

Pillar II Masterclass: Finding the Right People

n

A powerful brand is useless if it's not seen by the right people. This pillar measures your proficiency with LinkedIn's search tools to identify key decision-makers efficiently. It's not about connecting with everyone; it's about connecting with the *right* ones. High scorers are masters of advanced search, Boolean operators, and leveraging second-degree connections.

Pillar III Masterclass: Engaging with Insights

n

This is where you build your reputation as a thought leader. Engaging with insights means discovering and sharing relevant, timely content that resonates with your audience. It's measured by the engagement your posts receive (likes, comments, shares) and your participation in meaningful conversations. The goal is to become the go-to source for industry news and perspective.

Pillar IV Masterclass: Building Relationships

n

The final pillar is about converting connections into currency. This score reflects your ability to build trust and rapport within your network. It's measured by your connection acceptance rate, your response rate to messages, and the seniority of the people you're connecting with. The ultimate goal is to build a network of strong, authentic relationships that can lead to business opportunities.

The SSI Score Fallacy: Vanity Metric or Vital Sign?

n

It's a fair question: Does this score actually matter, or is it just another metric to chase? The answer depends on your approach. If you chase the score by spamming connections and low-value content, it's a vanity metric. But if you see it as a vital sign—a reflection of fundamentally sound sales activities—it becomes an invaluable guide for your professional development.

Frequently Asked Questions

n
n What is considered a 'good' SSI score?n

Generally, a score of 70 or above is considered good, and top performers in social selling often have scores above 80. However, context matters. It's most valuable to compare your score to your industry peers and your own team members, which LinkedIn Sales Navigator allows you to do.

n
n
n How often does the LinkedIn SSI score update?n

Your SSI score is typically updated daily. This allows you to see the direct impact of your activities on the platform relatively quickly, making it a responsive metric for tracking your social selling efforts.

n
n
n Do I need LinkedIn Sales Navigator to have an SSI score?n

No, you do not need a premium Sales Navigator account to view your basic SSI score. Anyone with a LinkedIn account can check their score for free at the official SSI dashboard link. However, Sales Navigator provides more detailed insights, such as how you rank against your industry and team.

n
n
n Does my SSI score directly affect my visibility in the LinkedIn feed?n

While LinkedIn's algorithm is complex and not fully public, there is a strong correlation. The activities that increase your SSI score—such as creating quality content, engaging meaningfully, and growing a relevant network—are the same activities the algorithm rewards with greater reach and visibility.

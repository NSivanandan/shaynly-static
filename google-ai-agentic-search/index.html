Frequently Asked Questions

n n
n Why is Google ignoring my Business Profile updates?n
n Google's AI has determined that external signals (what third-party sites say about you) are more trustworthy than self-reported data. The AI scrapes your website, Yelp, social media, and directories to construct your business identity, often overriding manual dashboard inputs entirely.n
n
nn
n What is Google Duplex and how does it affect my business?n
n Google Duplex is an AI system that makes phone calls to businesses using human-sounding voices to verifyn Google Duplex is an AI system that makes phone calls to businesses using human-sounding voices to verify information like hours, pricing, and services. When your staff answers these calls with vague responses ("it depends," "call for quote"), your business gets flagged as having low data confidence, which removes you from AI-powered recommendations.n
n
nn
n Can I still rank in traditional Google Search, or is everything AI now?n
n Traditional search results still exist, but they're increasingly being replaced by AI Mode recommendations and zero-click answers. By 2026, approximately 50% of local searches are answered without clicks to business websites. To survive, you must optimize for both: traditional SEO (for desktop users) and agentic search (for AI-first users).n
n
nn
n How do I know if Google's AI has scraped incorrect information about my business?n
n Open your Google Business Profile and check the "Services" tab. Look for small citation icons next to descriptions—these indicate AI-generated content. If the descriptions don't match your actual services or contain outdated information, it means the AI scraped incorrect data from external sources. Fix this by ensuring consistency across your website, Yelp, Facebook, and all directory listings.n
n
nn
n What's the difference between traditional SEO and "Agentic Search" optimization?n
n Traditional SEO optimizes for human visitors who click through to your website. Agentic Search optimizes for AI agents that need to answer user queries instantly without sending users to your site. This requires structured data (Schema markup), flat site architecture (0-1 click access to key info), and machine-readable pricing/service data. You need both strategies in 2026.n
n
nn
n My website was built with Elementor/Divi. Do I need to rebuild it completely?n
n Not necessarily. You can keep your visually designed pages for human visitors, but create separate "data pages" with clean HTML specifically for AI consumption. Alternatively, implement proper Schema markup and ensure your critical information (pricing, services, service areas) is in plain HTML, not JavaScript-rendered. The key is making AI's job easy—if pricing requires parsing 5,000 lines of code, you're invisible.n
n
nn
n How often should I post on social media for Google to extract content?n
n Aim for at least 3-4 posts per week across Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok. Google's AI actively monitors these platforms and automatically extracts content to populate your Business Profile. Focus on posts that mention services, pricing, special offers, and include photos/videos of your work. The fresher your social content, the more current your AI-generated profile becomes.n
n
nn
n What is Schema markup and do I really need it?n
n Schema markup is structured data code that tells AI agents exactly what your content means. Instead of the AI guessing that "$150" is a price, Schema explicitly declares it as an Offer with price, currency, and availability. In 2026, Schema isn't optional—it's the language AI agents speak. Without it, you're forcing AI to guess, which results in low confidence scores and invisibility.n
n
nn
n How do I measure if my agentic search optimization is working?n
n Track these metrics: (1) Google Business Profile impressions in AI Mode vs. traditional Search, (2) Direct calls/messages without website clicks (zero-click conversions), (3) Schema validation pass rate in Google Search Console, (4) Local pack rankings when users search with specific intent ("emergency plumber pricing"), (5) Social media content appearing in your GBP automatically.n
n
nn
n Is this just a Google thing, or are other platforms doing the same?n
n This is an industry-wide shift. Bing's AI search, ChatGPT's search features, and emerging AI assistants all prioritize structured, machine-readable data. By optimizing for Google's Agentic Search, you're simultaneously preparing for all AI-powered discovery platforms. The principles are universal: clean data, structured markup, and instant accessibility.n
n
nn
n What happens if I don't implement these changes?n
n Within 6-12 months, your business becomes increasingly invisible to AI-powered search recommendations. Users asking "Find me [your service] with pricing" will see competitors who have implemented agentic optimization. Your traditional website traffic will decline as more searches end in zero-click answers. Eventually, you'll be forced to rely entirely on expensive paid ads while competitors get organic AI-driven leads for free.n
